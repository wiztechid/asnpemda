# Rewarded Ads — Deferred Monetization Decision v1.0

Status: **PARKED / NOT ENABLED**. Implement only after a suitable rewarded-ad product is actually available, approved for this site, and compliant with its current policies. This is a product decision, not a claim that the feature or network is active.

## Core promise
- Articles, regulations, basic calculators, explanations, formulas, and single-transaction detailed PDF remain free.
- Never gate legal references, necessary verification information, or essential ASN work outputs behind advertising.
- Ads must not interfere with calculations or imply an official government endorsement.

## Optional rewarded unlock
- Only for genuinely optional enhancements such as bulk PDF/Excel export or batch processing.
- Explicit opt-in with a clear description of the reward, duration, and alternative path.
- No forced ad clicks, artificial impressions, hidden subscriptions, personal-data harvesting, or coercive CPA tasks.
- Reward is noncash, nontransferable and restricted to the platform, subject to the chosen provider's policy.
- Do not promise unlock until the ad provider verifies completion; handle unavailable inventory and errors without breaking the free tool.

## Activation gates
1. ASN Pemda content and core calculators are production-ready and legally reviewed.
2. Rewarded ads are supported and enabled on the publisher account and eligible for the site/region.
3. Review latest publisher policies, consent requirements, privacy notices and restrictions on incentives.
4. Test accessibility, mobile UX, error recovery, fraud resistance, and clear free alternatives.
5. Check conflict-of-interest boundaries for an ASN operator and ensure no official-government affiliation is implied.
6. Human GO decision; keep this feature off until then.

## Monetization priority
First: helpful free tools and trustworthy articles.
Second: unobtrusive contextual ads or relevant sponsorship.
Third: optional rewarded unlock for premium batch features when eligible.
No content locker for essential calculations or public legal information.
